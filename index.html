What we handle

Every service, under one roof

Certificates, ID cards, bill payments, exam services, printing and more — visit us or upload your documents online and we handle the paperwork so you don't have to chase government counters.

📜

Community Certificate

Caste and community certificates for education, government schemes and reservation benefits.

🏡

Nativity Certificate

Proof of origin for jobs, admissions and government applications requiring place-of-birth verification.

💰

Income Certificate

Income certificates for scholarships, bank loans, fee concessions and government welfare schemes.

🏅

OBC Certificate

Other Backward Class certificates for central and state government reservations and schemes.

🪪

New PAN Card

Fresh PAN card applications for individuals — processed quickly with minimal documents needed.

✏️

PAN Card Correction

Name, date of birth and address corrections on existing PAN cards with proper documentation.

🗳️

New Voter ID

New voter registration for first-time voters — only Aadhaar and a photo required.

🔄

Voter ID Correction

Name, address, photo and date-of-birth corrections on existing Voter ID / EPIC cards.

🛒

New Ration Card

New family ration card applications including all family member Aadhaar linking and gas bill linking.

💡

EB Bill Payment

Quick electricity bill payment for your home or shop — just bring your bill copy or consumer number.

🎓

College Fees / Exam Fee Payments

Pay college fees, semester fees and exam fees online without standing in campus queues.

📝

TNPSC Exam Services

Application filling, registration and support for TNPSC exams and government job applications.

🏢

MSME Registration / Update

New Udyam / MSME registration or updating existing business details for your enterprise.

🪪

PVC Card / Plastic Card Printing

Durable PVC plastic card printing for ID cards, Aadhaar, PAN and business cards.

📄

Certificate Lamination

Protect your certificates and documents with clean, bubble-free lamination.

📎

Spiral Binding

Neat spiral binding for projects, reports, resumes and important document sets.

🧾

PF Services

PF withdrawal, transfer and UAN-related support and application assistance.
